## Abstract
This paper explores the burgeoning field of algorithmic trading as it becomes increasingly accessible to retail investors. Historically the domain of institutional finance, modern advancements in open-source libraries, cloud computing, and user-friendly APIs have lowered the barrier to entry. We examine practical strategies, essential technical considerations, and accessible tools that empower individual investors to develop and deploy automated trading systems, moving beyond discretionary decision-making to data-driven execution.

## Introduction
The landscape of personal finance and investing is undergoing a significant transformation, driven by technology. Where once high-frequency trading and complex quantitative strategies were exclusively the purview of Wall Street firms, the democratization of financial data and computational power has opened doors for the individual investor. Algorithmic trading, which involves using computer programs to automate trading decisions and execution, offers a compelling advantage by removing emotional bias, enabling rapid analysis of market data, and executing trades at optimal moments. This paper delves into how a software engineer, with a foundational understanding of programming and data, can leverage these developments to build robust, automated investment strategies.
